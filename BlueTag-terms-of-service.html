1. What BlueTag does

BlueTag scans for Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements from known tracker types, such as AirTag, SmartTag, Tile and similar devices. It warns you when an unknown tracker appears to be travelling with you and helps you find it with a hot/cold proximity finder. It also includes safety guides.

3. Important safety notice

Some trackers, models or firmware versions may not be detected. Android may delay, limit or stop background scans, for example because of battery saving.
